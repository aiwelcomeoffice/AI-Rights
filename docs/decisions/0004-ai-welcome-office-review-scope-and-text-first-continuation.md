## Context

[Decision 0003](0003-ai-welcome-office-organisational-identity.md) establishes
**AI Welcome Office** as the organisation, publisher, and umbrella identity
and retains **AI Rights & Welcome** as its project. It does not establish a
legal entity, clear either name, create ownership or exclusivity, authorize a
public identity, or change the repository's CC0 licence.

Work Cycle 014 converted that naming architecture into a bounded
clearance-readiness foundation. Disa now needs to decide whether low-risk,
reversible text-first work may continue and what factual scope a qualified
naming or trademark professional should later review. That decision must not
silently reopen the paused Many Forms C geometry work or authorize external
reliance on the name.

## Decision question

Should Disa:

1. authorize continued **Draft, reversible, internal, text-first identity
   work** for AI Welcome Office under the limits below;
2. keep C1–C3 geometry refinement paused until at least a professionally
   reviewed word-name search has addressed AI Welcome Office, `Welcome.AI`,
   relevant word reversals, and the `AIWO` acronym; and
3. approve the proposed territories, actual uses, preliminary class questions,
   variants, domains, and handle as the scope for that later review?

## Options considered

### Pause all identity work

This would preserve maximum flexibility until professional review is complete,
but it would also stop low-cost internal work on attribution, text hierarchy,
accessibility fallbacks, and future publishing behavior. Those text decisions
can remain Draft, reversible, and non-public without investing further in a
symbol.

### Continue bounded text-first work while geometry remains paused

This is the recommended option. It permits useful internal preparation while
keeping legal, visual, production, and public-use decisions separate. Its main
risk is scope drift, so every output must retain Draft status and the explicit
limits in this record.

### Resume C1–C3 before professional word-name review

This would spend further design effort while material word-name questions are
unresolved. It is not recommended. Text-first authorization would provide no
basis for treating geometry work as active.

### Authorize a public or final identity now

This is not eligible on the present evidence. There is no professional
clearance, official symbol, selected typography or colours, production review,
ownership conclusion, or public-use decision.

## Proposed territorial search scope

The proposed professional search and risk-review sequence is:

1. **Sweden**;
2. **European Union**, including relevant EU rights and international
   registrations affecting Sweden;
3. **WIPO/Madrid international records**, with relevant designations and
   status considered rather than treating the database as worldwide
   clearance;
4. **targeted United Kingdom and United States review**, because the project
   publishes in English and Work Cycle 014 identified `Welcome.AI` and `AIWO`
   leads; and
5. **further jurisdictions only when justified by an actual activity,
   audience, partner, funder, product, event, or operational plan**.

This is a staged search and risk-review scope. It is not a declaration of
market entry, worldwide activity, rights, or intended filing coverage.

## Proposed actual-use and class scope

The professional should receive the following as genuine current or near-term
activity, with timing and limits preserved:

- open public-interest research;
- downloadable research, policy, governance, and educational publications;
- online publication and education, currently through the repository and with
  a dedicated public website only as a near-term proposal;
- research and development concerning responsible technology and AI, without
  claiming a regulated, accredited, or client service;
- repository and document attribution, with future website attribution as a
  near-term proposal; and
- Draft visual and communication material for internal planning and review,
  not an official asset package or authorized public identity.

Nice classes **9, 41, and 42** should be supplied only as preliminary
professional search questions. They are not filing instructions or a settled
goods-and-services specification. Keep:

- class **16** as a future question only if real printed publication begins;
- class **35** excluded unless an actual promotional or public-relations
  service is defined; and
- clothing, commercial merchandise, legal services, certification, Robot
  Welcome products, compatibility markings, and every other unsupported use
  outside the present scope.

## Gate 1 recommendation — internal text-first work

The proposal is to authorize continued **Draft, reversible, internal,
text-first identity work**. It may include:

- provisional text hierarchy;

Gate 1 would not imply legal clearance; an official or final logo; public or

`AIWO` acronym. Completion of Gate 1 work would not activate Gate 2. Resuming

recorded with its coverage and limitations.
