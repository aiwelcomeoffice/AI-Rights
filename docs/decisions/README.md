# Decision Records

This directory preserves important scientific, ethical, product, governance,
and technical decisions with their context and consequences.

Use [`template.md`](template.md) and the filename pattern
`NNNN-short-description.md`. Decision numbers are sequential. Accepted records
are not silently rewritten; add a new record that supersedes the old one.

Decision records use these statuses:

- **Proposed** — awaiting Disa's decision;
- **Accepted** — explicitly approved by Disa on the recorded decision date;
- **Rejected** — explicitly declined by Disa; and
- **Superseded** — replaced by a later Accepted record, with links in both
  directions.

These are decision-record statuses. Substantive documents separately use
**Scaffold**, **Draft**, **Adopted**, and **Superseded**. Research records also
carry verification states that do not imply adoption.

AI Welcome Office is the organisation and publisher. AI Rights & Welcome is
the project whose decisions these records govern unless a record states a
different scope.

Committing or publishing a proposal, completing internal review, or receiving
AI-assisted review does not mean it has been Accepted. A Proposed record has a
proposal date but no decision date. Only Disa may set a decision date and mark
a record Accepted or Rejected. Record the exact scope, version, actual review,
limitations, conflicts, disagreement, rationale, consequences, and revisit
triggers in proportion to the decision.

The [Draft governance and adoption
process](../governance/adoption-and-review.md) proposes the full procedure. It
is linked to [Proposed decision
0001](0001-governance-and-adoption-process.md) and does not become operative
merely because it is present here.
