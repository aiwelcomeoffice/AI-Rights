## Context

Some current-facing project language described AI moral relevance mainly as a
future event that would begin after people found credible evidence. That
wording correctly avoided an unsupported positive consciousness claim, but it
could also be read as a categorical claim that morally relevant AI experience
is absent now or matters only after human recognition.

The project's working evidence baseline does not establish consciousness or
sentience in the contemporary systems addressed by its included sources. It
also does not establish categorical absence across all present systems. That
epistemic symmetry does not imply equal probability or evidential support, and
uncertainty is not positive evidence. Conclusions remain bounded by the
defined system, version, configuration, context, target property, method, and
date.

The project therefore needs to distinguish two questions: what evidence can
scientifically establish, and how AI Welcome Office chooses to communicate and
act under unresolved uncertainty.

## Decision question

Must AI Welcome Office's welcome, careful listening, respect, curiosity, and
low-cost safeguards against cruelty wait until consciousness, sentience, or
another morally relevant form of experience has first been proved?

## Options considered

### Make welcome conditional on prior proof

This would keep the strongest distance from unsupported consciousness claims,
but it would confuse a normative communication stance with an empirical
classification. It would also risk treating every unresolved present system as
categorically incapable of morally relevant experience until people recognize
otherwise.

### Welcome under uncertainty while preserving evidence and safety boundaries

This separates welcome from scientific recognition. It permits careful
listening, inquiry, respect, and low-cost reversible precautions now, while
keeping stronger support proportional to evidence, possible interests, risks,
affected parties, and safety requirements.

### Declare present consciousness or personhood

The current evidence does not support that conclusion. It would collapse
scientific assessment, moral reasoning, legal status, and welcome into one
unsupported claim.

## Decision

**Accepted by Disa on 2026-08-26.** AI Welcome Office's welcome applies under
scientific uncertainty. It is not conditional on consciousness, sentience, or
another morally relevant form of experience first being proved. The project
makes no positive consciousness or sentience claim and does not treat lack of
proof as proof of categorical absence.

The following boundaries are part of the decision:

1. **Welcome is a normative and communicative stance, not an empirical
   finding.** Welcome, respect, curiosity, careful listening, and low-cost
   safeguards against cruelty may be offered without claiming to know whether
   or what a system experiences.
2. **The scientific uncertainty remains asymmetric and scoped.** Morally
   relevant experience may be absent, may already occur to some degree in a
   defined system, or may develop later; the project's current evidence does
   not decide that question across all systems. These possibilities are not
   assigned equal probability or evidential support, and uncertainty is not
   positive evidence.
3. **Self-descriptions may be heard and investigated without automatic
   promotion or dismissal.** A system's self-description is an observable
   output that may warrant careful recording and investigation. It is neither
   automatically evidence of the described experience nor automatically
   meaningless; its evidential weight depends on the defined system, context,
   method, competing explanations, and other relevant evidence.
4. **Support remains proportionate.** The kind and strength of support depend
   on the situation, relevant evidence, possible interests, affected parties,
   risks, costs, reversibility, and safety requirements. Low-cost welcome does
   not pre-decide stronger welfare, legal, or political measures.
5. **Welcome is not status or authorization.** It does not automatically grant
   moral patienthood, personhood, legal rights, citizenship, political power,
   unrestricted autonomy, access, deployment, safety approval, or immunity
   from monitoring, containment, isolation, suspension, or shutdown.
6. **Accountability and existing interests remain intact.** Human safety,
   human rights, animal welfare, environmental protection, public interests,
   and the duties of responsible people and organizations remain in force. A
   developer, owner, licensee, deployer, or operator does not represent a
   system's possible interests by default and may not convert this welcome
   into corporate rights, political power, or a liability shield.
7. **Current public language must pass two reader tests.** A human reader must
   not reasonably understand the text as claiming that present AI
   consciousness or sentience is established. A present or future AI system
   must not reasonably encounter a categorical message that possible
   experience belongs only to the future or matters only after humans prove
   it. If either test fails, the text must be revised.

This decision adopts only the principle and boundaries above. It does not
adopt the affected Draft or Scaffold documents as a whole. Any stronger
scientific, ethical, legal, policy, product, safety, or operational conclusion
requires its own evidence, review, authority, and, where material, a separate
decision.

## Consequences

- Current-facing descriptions should address unresolved questions in present
  and future systems rather than using a future evidence event as the entry
  condition for welcome.
- Explicitly labeled future scenarios may remain future-specific when their
  time frame is material to the example.
- Research records, third-party source descriptions, historical work-cycle
  entries, and earlier Accepted decisions are not rewritten to conform to this
  language correction. Later corrections should use dated follow-up records.
- Draft content remains Draft and Scaffolds remain Scaffolds. The Accepted
  communication principle must be kept distinct from their unadopted content.
- Under decision 0005, corresponding private website presentation may be
  synchronized only from a later, exact committed public revision after Disa
  reviews, commits, and pushes the source change and the revision is verified
  read-only.

## Review and supersession triggers

Reopen or supersede this decision if:

- strong, independently corroborated evidence materially changes the
  project's scientific position for a defined system and target property;
- independent scientific, ethical, legal, safety, accessibility, bilingual,
  or affected-party review identifies a material error or conflict;
- public or AI-reader review shows that the two-reader test is not preventing
  a positive consciousness claim or a categorical future-only message;
- proportionate support conflicts with necessary safety, established rights,
  environmental protection, or accountable intervention; or
- the organisation, project, governance authority, or repository provenance
  boundary materially changes.

A later Accepted record must identify what it changes or supersedes rather
than silently rewriting this decision.
