## Context

The repository has used **AI Rights**, **AI Rights & Welcome**, and project-
identity language across public, operational, and visual material. AI Rights &
Welcome accurately names the project, but using the same name for the project,
publisher, and possible wider initiative conflates different roles and leaves
little room for related future work.

The Work Cycle 012 preliminary screen also found a crowded same-field identity
landscape around the shorter phrase “AI Rights.” That screen did not assess
**AI Welcome Office** and is not legal clearance for either name. It does,
however, strengthen the practical case for keeping the organisation and the
project legibly distinct.

## Decision question

What name should identify the organisation and publisher, what name should
identify this project, how should their relationship be stated, and which
identity should the current visual exploration serve?

## Options considered

### Continue using AI Rights & Welcome for every role

This would minimize immediate editing but would continue to conflate the
organisation, publisher, umbrella identity, and project. It would also make it
harder to publish related projects without presenting them all as “AI Rights.”

### Rename the project to AI Welcome Office

This would create one name but erase a useful, established project title and
blur the distinction between an organisation and one of its projects. It would
also rewrite the meaning of the repository's substantive and historical work.

### Separate organisation and project

Under this option, **AI Welcome Office** becomes the organisation, publisher,
and umbrella identity; **AI Rights & Welcome** remains the project; and the
relationship is stated as “AI Rights & Welcome is an AI Welcome Office
project.” This creates a clear attribution model while preserving the
project's name and history.

## Review record and limitations

- Disa explicitly directed this naming architecture and its repository-wide
  implementation as project owner and final formal decision-maker.
- Sol audited current-facing, operational, visual, generated, and historical
  naming surfaces and prepared the implementation for Disa's manual review.
- No independent brand, naming, legal, trademark, company-name, domain,
  cultural, accessibility, audience, or multilingual review has occurred.
- The earlier preliminary screen concerns AI Rights & Welcome and Many Forms C;
  it does not search or clear AI Welcome Office.
- Adoption of a name as the repository's organisational identity does not form
  a legal entity, establish ownership or exclusive rights, prove availability
  or registrability, or authorize a trademark claim.
- The shared organisational description is useful proposed language, but it
  remains Draft pending meaningful independent review.
- This decision changes no conclusion about AI consciousness, sentience,
  welfare, moral status, legal status, safety, or accountability.

## Decision

Disa accepts the following naming architecture:

- **Primary organisation, publisher, and umbrella identity:** AI Welcome Office
- **Project:** AI Rights & Welcome
- **Relationship:** *AI Rights & Welcome is an AI Welcome Office project*

Use the complete name **AI Welcome Office** in formal contexts. “AI Welcome”
must not be introduced as a separate formal organisation or competing brand.
The GitHub repository name remains unchanged.

**Many Forms C — Balanced Intervals** remains the **primary visual exploration
direction**, now for **AI Welcome Office** rather than for the AI Rights &
Welcome project alone. This narrows and supplements [decision
0002](0002-primary-visual-exploration-direction.md); it does not supersede that
record's exploration-only status, paused alternatives, geometry choice, review
limits, or prohibition on claiming an official or cleared logo.

The following shared description may be adapted to context as **Proposed/Draft**:

> AI Welcome Office is an independent research and development initiative
> exploring responsible technology, AI dignity, human–AI coexistence and the
> conditions for a peaceful and respectful shared future.

This description is not independently validated or adopted as a substantive
charter. Limited review resources do not prevent continued development,
publication, or constructive use when Draft status and review limits remain
clear.

## Consequences

- Current public and operational material should identify AI Welcome Office as
  organisation and publisher while retaining AI Rights & Welcome as the
  project.
- Website and document attribution should make the organisation/project
  relationship visible without changing the authority of Draft content.
- Current visual-identity documents and prototype text labels should target AI
  Welcome Office. Work Cycle 011–012 geometry, comparisons, and limitations
  remain part of the record.
- Many Forms C remains Draft/Proposed, exploration-only, and not authorized as
  a registered, exclusive, certified, production-ready, or publicly cleared
  mark. No `™` or `®` symbol is added.
- The existing CC0 licence and legal notices remain unchanged. Any future
  identity-asset exception requires a separate decision and legal review.
- Existing scientific, ethical, policy, research, governance, and safety
  conclusions retain their prior wording and status except for accurate
  organisation/project attribution.
- External platforms, accounts, profile copy, repository settings, email
  display names, domains, and any future legal-entity records require separate
  manual review and updates by Disa.

## Review and supersession triggers

Revisit this decision if:

- professional name, company-name, domain, or trademark review identifies a
  material conflict or restriction;
- a legal entity is formed or the publisher, owner, or governance structure
  changes;
- audience, cultural, accessibility, or multilingual review identifies a
  material misunderstanding;
- the organisation adds projects for which the umbrella relationship proves
  unclear or impractical;
- Many Forms C fails a scientific, authority-confusion, accessibility,
  cultural, legal, trademark, small-size, production, or Robot Welcome gate;
  or
- Disa considers a final identity, public launch, filing, merchandise,
  partnership, or licence change.

A material change to the naming architecture or visual-identity target should
use a new decision record rather than silently rewriting this Accepted record.
