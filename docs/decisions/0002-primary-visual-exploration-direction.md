## Context

Work Cycle 011 created an equal, black-and-white comparison of three Open
Threshold and three Many Forms, Common Ground variants. It did not select a
winner. After reviewing those prototypes, Disa and ChatGPT preferred **Many
Forms C — Balanced Intervals** and asked Sol to assess it independently rather
than being assigned the same view.

Disa's first response is preserved verbatim:

> The human brain immediately asks, “Is it a periscope?” It is uneven, it
> grabs attention, and that feels perfect.

The preference concerns a promising direction for further inquiry, not an
official identity. An early rights and identity-landscape screen was required
before additional design investment.

## Decision question

Which Work Cycle 011 prototype, if any, should become the primary internal
exploration direction, and what should happen to the other directions?

## Options considered

### Continue all six equally

This would preserve breadth but spend time on directions that the current
collaborators find less promising. It would also delay the early rights screen.

### Advance Many Forms C and preserve the others as paused references

This retains the strongest current combination of curiosity, asymmetry,
distinct forms, intervals, common ground, warmth, and application breadth
without deleting the comparison history. It does not assume that the forms
represent people, AI systems, institutions, conscious subjects, or moral
categories.

### Select or adopt Many Forms C as the project logo

The evidence and review are insufficient. There has been no independent
audience, accessibility, cultural, legal, trademark, or production review, and
the preliminary identity screen found same-field name issues requiring
attention.

## Review record and limitations

- Disa reviewed the Work Cycle 011 prototypes as project owner and recorded
  her preference for Many Forms C.
- ChatGPT independently preferred Many Forms C and identified surveillance,
  military-optics, robot-sensor, skyline, and corporate-monogram readings for
  testing.
- Sol independently prefers Many Forms C among the six prototypes. In Sol's
  assessment, its irregular asymmetry creates useful visual tension; the three
  unlike forms remain distinct while their intervals and shared baseline make
  relationship visible without merger. It is the strongest current bridge
  between a serious civic identity and a warmer, more playful editorial
  system. Sol's reservation is that the left and central geometry can first
  read as a periscope, sensor, skyline, or corporate monogram. Those are
  unresolved hypotheses, not audience findings.
- This agreement is an **internal human–AI collaborative consensus**. It is not
  independent external review, public consultation, audience testing,
  scientific evidence, legal clearance, accessibility validation, or cultural
  review.
- No present AI consciousness, absence of consciousness, preference,
  experience, or moral status is inferred from this collaboration or the
  abstract forms.
- The linked preliminary screen is research support, not legal advice. Its
  name findings trigger a cautious pause in new refinements under the Work
  Cycle 012 instruction.

No collaborator recorded disagreement about the exploration preference. The
shared reasons differ in emphasis, and the unresolved misreadings remain part
of the record.

## Decision

Disa accepts the following **exploration-only** disposition:

| Work Cycle 011 direction or variant | Work Cycle 012 status |
| --- | --- |
| **Many Forms C — Balanced Intervals** | **Primary exploration direction** |
| Many Forms A — Shared Baseline | Paused reference |
| Many Forms B — Open Field | Paused reference |
| Open Threshold C — Soft Canopy | Paused warm cross-direction reference; not advanced because its visual territory appears crowded and its apparent property, hospitality, religious, access, corporate-brand, and trademark risks are higher |
| Open Threshold A — Offset Frame | Paused reference |
| Open Threshold B — Split Lintel | Paused reference |
| Reciprocal Signal | Remains paused as a primary identity direction; its question–evidence, claim–source, and accountability relationships may still inform later editorial work |

Paused means preserved for reference, not rejected or deleted. Work Cycle 011
remains the equal-comparison cycle and is not rewritten as a selection.

“Primary exploration direction” authorizes focused internal study only. It
does not make Many Forms C:

- the official project logo or an adopted identity;
- a preferred refinement selected from later descendants;
- trademark-cleared, registrable, available, or legally safe;
- protected by an established copyright or exclusive right;
- accessibility-tested or culturally reviewed;
- approved for merchandise or Robot Welcome; or
- ready or authorized for production, filing, publication, or public use.

The preliminary rights screen found apparently close **names in relevant
fields**. In accordance with the Work Cycle 012 stop condition, no C1, C2, or
C3 SVG descendant is created. Further geometry may resume only after Disa
decides how to address the name landscape and the scope of professional
follow-up.

## Consequences

- The original
  [`many-forms-c-balanced-intervals.svg`](../brand/prototypes/many-forms-c-balanced-intervals.svg)
  remains unchanged as the Work Cycle 011 reference.
- All older prototype sheets, the native-size contact sheet, and their
  evaluation history remain intact.
- New visual effort is paused after documenting the intended controlled tests;
  color, typography, polished applications, and production assets remain out
  of scope.
- A Draft identity and trademark policy proposal now separates open
  educational/research reuse from questions about future official identity
  assets. It changes no licence by itself.
- The project's provisional name and any future symbol need professional,
  use-specific review before filing, launch, merchandise, partnership, or
  other reliance.

## Review and supersession triggers

Revisit this decision if:

- professional advice, a fuller register search, or a third-party contact
  changes the name or visual-risk assessment;
- the project changes its name, owner, legal entity, jurisdictions, goods,
  services, audience, or intended uses;
- unprompted human or separately identified AI-assisted review shows material
  surveillance, military, sensor, hierarchy, authority, or overclaim readings;
- accessibility, cultural, small-size, production, or Robot Welcome separation
  testing produces a failed gate;
- a later refinement preserves the concept more effectively; or
- Disa considers adoption or public use.

A later change of primary exploration direction, identity adoption, or
production/public-use approval requires its own scoped decision record rather
than a silent edit to this Accepted record.
