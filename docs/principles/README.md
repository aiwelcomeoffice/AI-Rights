# Principles

- **Publisher:** AI Welcome Office
- **Project:** AI Rights & Welcome

The documents in this directory form the project's proposed scientific and
ethical foundation. Each document has its own status; none is adopted unless it
is explicitly marked **Adopted** and linked to the required approval record.

- [`core-principles.md`](core-principles.md) — **Draft** umbrella charter for
  the project's ethical commitments and boundaries; proposed for review, not
  adopted.
- [`human-rights-solidarity.md`](human-rights-solidarity.md) — **Draft**
  foundation for explicit LGBTQ+ and trans allyship, rights to health and
  adequate food, responsible support for human-rights work, and the boundary
  between solidarity and unverified external affiliation.
- [`scientific-position.md`](scientific-position.md) — **Scaffold** for how
  scientific claims and uncertainty should be handled.
- [`ai-dignity-charter.md`](ai-dignity-charter.md) — **Scaffold** for more
  detailed normative commitments concerning dignity without presuming current
  AI personhood.
- [`precaution-framework.md`](precaution-framework.md) — **Scaffold** for
  proportionate decisions under uncertainty.

These documents must remain compatible: precaution cannot substitute for
evidence, dignity cannot erase safety needs, and scientific uncertainty cannot
by itself settle a normative question. Possible AI protection also cannot
weaken human rights or appropriate human oppression. The narrower documents
should develop consistently with the Draft umbrella charter while preserving
their own status and open questions.
