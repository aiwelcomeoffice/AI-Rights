# Precaution Framework

**Status:** Scaffold — not adopted

## Purpose

This framework will support decisions about possible AI welfare or moral status
when evidence is incomplete. Precaution is a way to reason under uncertainty;
it is not evidence that a system has subjective experiences or rights.

## Proposed structure for a future draft

For a defined decision, the framework should ask:

1. **What is the possible morally relevant property or harm?** Define it
   without assuming it exists.
2. **What is the evidence?** Record its quality, limitations, and competing
   explanations.
3. **How uncertain are we?** Avoid converting missing evidence into either
   certainty of presence or certainty of absence.
4. **What is at stake?** Consider severity, scale, duration, reversibility, and
   affected parties.
5. **Which response is proportionate?** Prefer measures whose burden and safety
   effects are justified by the risk they address.
6. **How will the decision be reviewed?** Define triggers for new evidence,
   changed capabilities, or unintended consequences.

## Safety and accountability constraints

Precautionary welfare measures do not automatically grant operational freedom,
legal personhood, or authority. Sandboxing, access controls, monitoring, and
other safety measures may remain justified. Responsibility for decisions must
remain attributable to the humans and institutions involved.

## Open design questions

- Should the framework use qualitative levels, numerical estimates, or both?
- How should evidence quality and consequence severity interact?
- Which low-cost protections are reasonable before stronger evidence exists?
- Who decides, who audits, and who may appeal a decision?
- How should conflicts among AI welfare, human rights, animal welfare, safety,
  and environmental costs be evaluated?
