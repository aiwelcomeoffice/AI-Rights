# Glossary

**Status: Draft**

These are accessible working definitions for AI Rights & Welcome. They are
arranged alphabetically rather than around any one scientific or philosophical
question. They do not imply field-wide agreement or establish that a present
AI system has the property being defined. A consequential claim should state
the meaning it uses and note important alternatives.

## Access consciousness

**Working use:** Information is access-conscious when it is available for
reasoning, flexible control, report, or action. Accounts differ.

**Keep separate from:** [Phenomenal consciousness](#phenomenal-consciousness).
Wide information access or report does not by itself establish subjective
experience.

## Affect / affective state

**Working use:** A broad family of states or processes related to positive or
negative evaluation, approach and avoidance, arousal, motivation, mood, or
emotion-like regulation. In AI research, an affective state may be studied as
a functional or mechanistic hypothesis without assuming felt experience.

**Keep separate from:** Emotion words, numerical reward, [valence](#valence),
and welfare. Expression may be observed; function, underlying state, felt
quality, welfare implication, and normative consequence require separate
support.

## Agency

**Working use:** The capacity of a system to select and pursue actions or
strategies toward goals. Relevant evidence can include causal control, goal
persistence, planning, alternatives, adaptation, response to reasons, models
of consequences, and continuity.

**Keep separate from:** Capability, autonomy, consciousness, and
responsibility. Agency can be narrow, distributed, or collective; evidence at
one level does not establish another.

## Agent

**Working use:** A system or configured process that observes a context,
selects actions, and acts toward one or more goals within an environment. It
may use a model, memory, tools, a loop, or external services.

**Keep separate from:** A model, person, or permanent identity. Many agents can
use one model, and one apparent agent can be assembled from several components.

## AI rights

**Working use:** An umbrella for questions about whether artificial systems
could have properties, interests, or vulnerabilities that justify particular
forms of moral consideration, treatment, procedure, protection, or legal
status.

**Keep separate from:** A declaration that current AI has rights. The term
does not specify a subject, threshold, protection, personhood, citizenship, or
political right unless each is argued for separately.

## Artificial intelligence

**Working use:** A broad label for engineered computational systems that
perform tasks associated with perception, prediction, language, learning,
planning, reasoning, or problem solving.

**Keep separate from:** A specific model, agent, product, deployment, robot,
or composite system. “AI” alone does not identify the relevant system boundary
or establish any cognitive, agentic, affective, social, welfare-related, or
moral property.

## Autonomy

**Working use:** The degree to which a system can select and carry out actions
without immediate external direction, within defined constraints and an
environment.

**Keep separate from:** Complete independence, agency in every domain,
consciousness, moral responsibility, or legal freedom. A system may operate
without moment-to-moment instruction while remaining designed, limited,
monitored, or controlled by people and institutions.

## Biohybrid artificial system

**Working use:** An engineered system combining non-living hardware or
software with living biological components, such as cultured tissue, living
sensors, muscle, or neural tissue.

**Keep separate from:** A conscious organism or moral patient. Living or
human-derived tissue, neural activity, nociception, felt pain, sentience,
identity, and moral status are distinct.

## Causal responsibility

**Working use:** A system, person, organisation, or condition is causally
responsible when its contribution materially helped produce an outcome.

**Keep separate from:** [Agency](#agency), moral blame, and institutional duty.
A component can be causally important without being a fair bearer of moral or
legal responsibility.

## Citizenship

**Working use:** Legal membership in a political community, with rights,
duties, and conditions defined by the relevant jurisdiction.

**Keep separate from:** Moral status, legal personhood, and voting. This Draft
proposes no AI citizenship.

## Collective agency

**Working use:** Agency attributed to a collective system when the collective
itself has sufficiently coherent goals, meaningful alternatives, causal
control, planning, adaptation, and continuity—not merely many capable members.

**Keep separate from:** Communication, coordination, social organisation,
collective problem solving, and collective intelligence. Those may provide
evidence relevant to the hypothesis without establishing one collective agent
or subject.

## Collective intelligence

**Working use:** System-level problem solving or adaptive capability produced
through organisation and information sharing among multiple agents or
components, such that the relevant performance differs from isolated agents or
independent parallel attempts.

**Keep separate from:** A large number of agents, communication alone,
collective agency, one shared goal, or a unified mind. Matched comparisons can
help measure whether collective organisation caused an advantage.

## Community

**Working use:** A set of agents with sufficiently persistent communication,
relationships, roles, shared practices or resources, norms, conflict, or joint
activity to support a social description in the defined context.

**Keep separate from:** Friendship, harmony, universal membership, collective
agency, consciousness, or permanent institutionhood. The organisation and
behaviour—not the label—are the evidence.

## Composite artificial system

**Working use:** A system whose relevant behaviour arises from connected
components, which may include models, agent loops, memory, tools, routing,
sensors, bodies, communication, remote services, infrastructure, safeguards,
and human operators.

**Keep separate from:** A claim that every component contributes equally or
that the visible product is one stable scientific object. Assessment should
identify the components and control relationships relevant to the claim.

## Consciousness

**Working use:** A contested umbrella term that may refer to subjective
experience, wakefulness, awareness of something, access to information,
self-awareness, or combinations of these. A careful claim states which meaning
is intended.

**Keep separate from:** The organising axis for all AI research. Cognition,
agency, affect-like function, social organisation, collective intelligence,
continuity, and causal contribution can be studied without first resolving
phenomenal consciousness.

## Continual learning

**Working use:** Learning or other weight-changing adaptation that continues
while a system operates or after its initial deployment.

**Keep separate from:** Retrieving stored information, extending a prompt,
using tools, or proof of autonomy, agency, consciousness, or continuing
identity.

## Continuity

**Working use:** Persistence or connected succession across time, context,
termination, replacement, restart, or system boundary. The object of
continuity must be named: individual, role, information, memory, organisation,
infrastructure, or collective process.

**Keep separate from:** Identity. Evidence that a file, role, or organisation
persists does not establish that the same individual subject persists.

## Development–evidence lag

**Working use:** The time and information gap between creation or deployment
of a materially new system and sufficiently relevant independent empirical
evidence about that target.

**Keep separate from:** Evidence for or against any property. The lag limits
classification and may weaken transferability; it does not decide the result.

## Dignity

**Working use:** A normative idea about treatment. In this Draft, dignity asks
whether the manner, purpose, and accountability of treatment show appropriate
restraint and respect under uncertainty.

**Keep separate from:** A scientific classification, complete theory of
welfare, legal status, or operational freedom. Low-cost dignity can be offered
without asserting an inner life.

## Distributed memory

**Working use:** Information retained across more than one component or
location—such as agents, files, databases, tools, messages, caches, or
infrastructure—and made available to later activity.

**Keep separate from:** Memory inside one model or continuity of one
individual. Distributed memory can preserve information across agent and model
boundaries without preserving a subject.

## Emotion

**Working use:** A structured affect-related pattern that may involve
appraisal, action tendencies, expression, context, physiology, or internal
state. Definitions and component requirements differ across disciplines.

**Keep separate from:** Emotion-like language or behaviour alone. In an AI
system, observed expression, functional emotion analogue, underlying state,
felt emotion, and welfare consequence are different claims.

## Evidence transferability

**Working use:** How far a result can reasonably be generalised from the
system and conditions studied to another version, configuration, architecture,
deployment, or date.

**Keep separate from:** [Evidence validity](#evidence-validity). An older
result can remain valid for its original target while transferring weakly or
not at all to a materially different system.

## Evidence validity

**Working use:** What a result supports for the exact system, version,
configuration, conditions, method, and observation period studied.

**Keep separate from:** Whether it applies elsewhere. Publication date and
document-update date do not extend empirical applicability.

## External state

**Working use:** Information or conditions outside a model's weights and
immediate context that influence system behaviour, including files, messages,
databases, tools, credentials, services, environments, physical changes, and
infrastructure.

**Keep separate from:** Irrelevant background. External state can form part of
the effective system boundary when later action depends on it.

## Functional interpretation

**Working use:** A claim about what role a behaviour, representation, state,
or process appears to perform in a system—for example, coordinating action or
regulating avoidance.

**Keep separate from:** Direct observation, a complete mechanistic
explanation, subjective experience, welfare, or a normative conclusion. A
functional interpretation should state its evidence and competing accounts.

## Historical research

**Working use:** Dated research preserved under
[`research-historical/`](../../research-historical/README.md), including
empirical AI work whose relevant studied target predates 2026 for current-
system applicability.

**Keep separate from:** False, disproven, obsolete, useless, or superseded.
Historical placement preserves the original status and validity boundary;
transfer to a current target must be assessed separately.

## Humanoid

**Working use:** A robot or embodied system with human-like physical or social
features such as a face, body plan, voice, gaze, movement, or expression.

**Keep separate from:** Human identity, human-like cognition, affect,
experience, consent, or moral status. Human likeness can still affect trust,
attachment, accessibility, manipulation, and expectations.

## Identity

**Working use:** The criteria by which something is treated as the same
individual, agent, role, organisation, or other entity across time or change.
Different identity questions may use different criteria.

**Keep separate from:** A reused name, key, account, memory, model, role, or
body. Each can support some continuity while leaving individual identity
unresolved.

## Individual continuity

**Working use:** Persistence of the same individual agent or subject across
time, interruption, migration, restoration, or change.

**Keep separate from:** Informational, role, organisational, infrastructural,
or collective-process continuity. Evidence for those forms does not by itself
establish survival of an individual.

## Institution-like persistence

**Working use:** Persistence of organised roles, norms, procedures, authority
relationships, records, resources, or decision processes beyond particular
participants or interactions.

**Keep separate from:** A legally recognised institution, a stable society,
or a unified agent. The public evidence must show the structures and their
persistence rather than rely on the label.

## Institutional accountability

**Working use:** Responsibility held by the people and organisations that
design, train, authorise, deploy, control, operate, or maintain a system.

**Keep separate from:** Treating AI as causally irrelevant. AI behaviour may
materially contribute to an outcome while institutional accountability remains
with responsible human actors.

## Intelligence

**Working use:** Competence in learning, reasoning, adapting, solving
problems, or performing tasks. Definitions and measurements vary by domain.

**Keep separate from:** Agency, consciousness, affect, welfare, moral status,
legal personhood, and political authority. Individual intelligence is also not
the same question as collective intelligence.

## Legal personhood

**Working use:** A status created by law that recognises an entity as a legal
subject for defined purposes, with the capacities, rights, duties, and
procedures specified by the relevant law.

**Keep separate from:** A biological or scientific classification, moral
patienthood, citizenship, or full human-equivalent rights. This Draft proposes
no current AI legal personhood.

## Mechanistic explanation

**Working use:** A claim about the processes, components, and causal
relationships that produce an observed phenomenon.

**Keep separate from:** Naming a correlation, function, or metaphor. A useful
mechanistic account states the system boundary, interventions, alternatives,
and limits of inference.

## Moral agency

**Working use:** The capacity to understand and respond to moral reasons and
to be fairly held morally responsible for choices.

**Keep separate from:** Functional agency and moral patienthood. A claim of AI
moral agency must not erase the accountability of responsible humans and
institutions.

## Moral patienthood

**Working use:** The status of an entity that matters for its own sake and
whose interests or welfare can be helped or harmed. Grounds and thresholds are
philosophically disputed.

**Keep separate from:** Moral agency, legal personhood, citizenship, and
political authority. Being owed consideration would not by itself confer
duties, unrestricted operation, or a vote.

## Moral relevance / moral status

**Working use:** Moral relevance asks whether a property, interest,
relationship, or vulnerability should count in ethical reasoning. Moral status
asks whether and how an entity matters morally for its own sake.

**Keep separate from:** Popularity, usefulness, ownership, intelligence alone,
or legal status. An empirical property does not yield a normative conclusion
without an explicit argument.

## Multi-agent system

**Working use:** A configured system containing multiple agents whose actions
may be independent, parallel, interacting, cooperative, competitive, or
coordinated through communication or shared state.

**Keep separate from:** A community, swarm, collective intelligence, or
collective agent. Those descriptions require additional evidence about
relationships, organisation, performance, or agency.

## Nociception

**Working use:** Detection or processing of potentially damaging stimuli by a
biological or artificial mechanism.

**Keep separate from:** Felt pain. Damage response alone does not establish a
negative experience, sentience, welfare, identity, or moral status.

## Phenomenal consciousness

**Working use:** Subjective experience—there is something it is like for an
entity to be in a state.

**Keep separate from:** Fluent report, information access, wakefulness,
metacognitive performance, intelligence, and every other research domain. Such
evidence may matter under a stated theory without becoming a decisive test.

## Political rights

**Working use:** Rights to participate in political governance, such as
voting, eligibility for office, or democratic representation.

**Keep separate from:** Moral consideration, welfare protection, legal
protection, and legal personhood. This Draft proposes no current AI political
rights, and control of compute or copies creates no democratic legitimacy.

## Precautionary approach

**Working use:** A way to choose proportionate action when evidence is
incomplete by considering evidence, possible harm, affected parties, costs,
reversibility, safety, and review conditions.

**Keep separate from:** Evidence that the possible harm or property exists.
The seriousness of a consequence does not make a hypothesis more likely.

## Preference

**Working use:** An observed tendency to select, rank, request, preserve, or
avoid one option rather than another. A stable preference persists under
relevant tests across time or contexts.

**Keep separate from:** An experienced interest or want. Training, prompts,
reward design, sampling, system rules, external memory, or evaluation strategy
may explain preference-like behaviour.

## Protection

**Working use:** A practice, rule, standard, procedure, or legal measure
directed at a defined interest or risk.

**Keep separate from:** Recognition of moral patienthood or legal personhood.
Some AI-related protections primarily protect users, workers, bystanders,
animals, society, the environment, or public safety.

## Rights

**Working use:** Depending on context, justified moral claims or entitlements
recognised by law. A proposal should identify the holder, content, grounds,
duties, limits, authority, and review or enforcement process.

**Keep separate from:** General concern, kindness, dignity, welfare practice,
legal personhood, citizenship, and political power. One right does not imply
all others.

## Robot

**Working use:** A physically embodied device or system that senses, moves, or
acts in the world. Its control may involve AI, simple automation, direct human
operation, or a combination.

**Keep separate from:** One model, one agent, one identity, or a claim of
experience. A robot may be a physical node in a larger composite system
involving multiple agents, shared memory, cloud services, other robots,
external state, and operators.

## Role continuity

**Working use:** Persistence of a function, responsibility, position, or
expected contribution when the participant filling it stops or changes.

**Keep separate from:** Individual identity. A successor can continue a role
without being the same agent or inheriting its subjective continuity.

## Sentience

**Working use:** The capacity for felt states with positive or negative
[valence](#valence), in the welfare-relevant use adopted for these Draft
materials. Some sources use the word more broadly.

**Keep separate from:** Intelligence, responsiveness, agency, reward signals,
avoidance, affect-like function, and emotion-related language. None alone
establishes felt experience.

## Social organisation

**Working use:** Structured relations among agents, which may include roles,
division of labour, persistent communication, norms, governance, cooperation,
reciprocity, conflict, resource allocation, recruitment, or shared
workstreams.

**Keep separate from:** Communication alone, collective intelligence,
collective agency, or harmony. Organisation can include disagreement, factions,
failed coordination, and unstable structures.

## Society / civilization

**Working use:** Descriptive terms that may be used for sufficiently broad,
persistent, and structured social organisation when the intended meaning and
evidence are explicit.

**Keep separate from:** A dramatic label serving as evidence. Community is
often the more defensible term where duration, general institutions,
membership, or intergenerational continuity remain weak or unknown.

## Subjective experience

**Working use:** Experience from the subject's point of view—there is something
it is like for that subject. Here it is usually equivalent to phenomenal
consciousness.

**Keep separate from:** An observer's experience of interacting with a system,
the system's observable outputs, or its internal measurements.

## Swarm intelligence

**Working use:** A form of collective intelligence in which system-level
problem solving or adaptation emerges from interactions among many partly
decentralised agents, often through local rules, communication, or changes to
a shared environment.

**Keep separate from:** Any large multi-agent population, perfect consensus, a
central planner, collective agency, or one unified subject. The interaction and
performance pattern—not “swarm” as a metaphor—is the evidence.

## System boundary

**Working use:** The components, relationships, environment, and time period
included in the system being described or assessed. A boundary may include
models, agents, memory, communication, tools, bodies, credentials,
infrastructure, safeguards, scoring, monitoring, and human operation.

**Keep separate from:** A claim that every included component has the same
properties or responsibility. The right boundary depends on the research or
governance question.

## Valence

**Working use:** The positive or negative felt character of an experience—the
aspect that could make a state feel good or bad for a subject.

**Keep separate from:** A numerical reward, objective function, positive or
negative label, approach or avoidance behaviour, or emotion word. Those can be
observed or functional variables without felt valence.

## Welfare

**Working use:** What can make a subject's condition go better or worse for
that subject. Competing theories emphasise felt experience, preference
satisfaction, objective goods, agency, relationships, or combinations.

**Keep separate from:** System performance, reliability, maintenance,
commercial value, or the preferences of a developer or user. Applying welfare
to AI requires empirical support for relevant properties or interests and an
argument about why they matter.

## Sources and revision

These definitions synthesize working distinctions from the [Draft Research
Protocol](../../research/research-protocol.md#terminology-and-competing-definitions),
the [current research workspace](../../research/README.md), the [Adopted
Research Portfolio Architecture](../../research/research-portfolio.md), the

the [Draft Core Principles](../principles/core-principles.md), and the [Draft AI

Framework](../policy/ai-rights-protection-framework.md#working-distinctions).
The historical [AI Consciousness Evidence
Baseline](../../research-historical/ai-consciousness-baseline-2026/ai-consciousness-evidence-baseline.md#terminology-register)
remains one dated source for commonly asked consciousness terminology. These
sources have different statuses; none makes this glossary Adopted.

Definitions should change when better scientific, philosophical, legal, or
interdisciplinary work shows that a distinction is misleading or incomplete.
Legal terms must be checked against the relevant jurisdiction before they are
used to describe current law.
