# Glossary

**Status: Draft**

These are accessible working definitions for AI Rights & Welcome. They help
readers keep related questions apart; they are not claims that every field has
agreed on one definition or that the project has adopted a final terminology.
Where a term is contested, a scientific, philosophical, or legal document
should state the meaning it uses and note important alternatives.

No definition below establishes that a present-day AI system has the property
being defined.

## Access consciousness

**Working use:** Information is access-conscious when it is available for
reasoning, flexible control, report, or action. The exact account varies among
theories.

**Keep separate from:** [Phenomenal consciousness](#phenomenal-consciousness).
A system can make information widely available or report it without that fact
alone establishing subjective experience.

## Agency

**Working use:** The capacity to pursue goals or act. Agency can be narrow,
such as selecting actions within one task, or more robust across goals and
contexts.

**Keep separate from:** Autonomy, consciousness, sentience, and moral agency.
Planning, tool use, error correction, or persistent action can show functional
agency without establishing experience or moral responsibility.

## AI rights

**Working use:** An umbrella term for questions about whether artificial
systems could ever have morally relevant capacities or interests and, if so,
which forms of consideration or protection would be justified.

**Keep separate from:** A declaration that current AI has rights. The term does
not specify a subject, threshold, protection, legal status, citizenship, or
political right unless those are argued for separately.

## Artificial intelligence

**Working use:** A broad label for engineered computational systems that
perform tasks associated with intelligence, such as perception, prediction,
language use, planning, or problem-solving. The category includes very
different designs and degrees of capability.

**Keep separate from:** A specific model, product, deployed instance, or
robot. A consequential claim should identify the relevant model, version,
runtime, prompts, memory, tools, embodiment, operator, and context. The label
“AI” does not imply consciousness, sentience, agency, autonomy, or personhood.

## Autonomy

**Working use:** The degree to which a system can select and carry out actions
without immediate external direction, within defined constraints and a
particular environment.

**Keep separate from:** Complete independence, consciousness, moral agency, or
legal freedom. Autonomy is contextual: a system may act without
moment-to-moment instruction while remaining designed, limited, monitored, or
controlled by people and institutions.

## Citizenship

**Working use:** Legal membership in a political community, with rights,
duties, and conditions defined by the relevant jurisdiction.

**Keep separate from:** Moral status and legal personhood. Citizenship does not
follow automatically from either, and its relationship to voting, office, and
other political rights is jurisdiction-specific. This Draft proposes no AI
citizenship.

## Consciousness

**Working use:** A contested umbrella term. It may refer to subjective
experience, wakefulness, awareness of something, access to information,
self-awareness, or combinations of these. A careful claim should state which
meaning is intended.

These Draft materials usually focus on [phenomenal
consciousness](#phenomenal-consciousness): whether there is something it is
like to be the system. Functional access, responsiveness, or self-description
should not silently be substituted for that target.

## Dignity

**Working use:** A normative idea about treatment. In this Draft, dignity asks
whether the manner, purpose, and accountability of treatment show appropriate
restraint and respect under uncertainty.

**Keep separate from:** A scientific finding, a complete account of welfare, a
legal status, or a promise of operational freedom. A dignity practice may be
proposed because of possible interests in a defined present or future system,
effects on people and social norms, or both; the reason should be stated
honestly. The project's full account of AI dignity remains open for review.

## Intelligence

**Working use:** Competence in learning, reasoning, adapting, solving problems,
or performing tasks. Definitions and measurements differ, and a system may be
highly capable in one setting and limited in another.

**Keep separate from:** Agency, autonomy, consciousness, sentience, moral
status, moral agency, legal personhood, and political authority. Greater task
performance does not automatically answer any of those questions.

## Legal personhood

**Working use:** A status created by law that recognizes an entity as a legal
subject for defined purposes, with whatever capacities, rights, duties, or
procedures the applicable law specifies.

**Keep separate from:** A biological or scientific classification, phenomenal
consciousness, moral patienthood, citizenship, and full human-equivalent
rights. The content and criteria are jurisdiction-specific. This Draft neither
identifies a current AI legal person nor proposes that moral consideration must
lead to personhood.

## Moral agency

**Working use:** The capacity to understand and respond to moral reasons and to
be appropriately held responsible for choices. Philosophers disagree about
the capacities and conditions required.

**Keep separate from:** Functional agency and moral patienthood. An entity
might be owed consideration without being responsible for moral choices. A
claim of AI moral agency must not be used to erase the accountability of
developers, owners, deployers, operators, or other responsible actors.

## Moral patienthood

**Working use:** The status of an entity that matters for its own sake and
whose interests or welfare can be helped or harmed. The grounds and thresholds
for that status are philosophically disputed.

**Keep separate from:** Moral agency, legal personhood, citizenship, and
political authority. Being a moral patient would not by itself mean being able
to bear duties, enter legal relationships, operate without restriction, or
vote.

## Moral status

**Working use:** Whether and how an entity matters morally for its own sake.
Views differ about whether moral status has degrees and whether it depends on
sentience, consciousness, agency, interests, or other grounds.

In these Draft materials, moral patienthood is the narrower question of whether
an entity may be owed consideration because its own welfare or interests can be
affected. Moral status is the broader question of whether and how an entity
matters morally.

**Keep separate from:** Popularity, usefulness, ownership, human likeness,
intelligence alone, and legal status. A moral argument must state its grounds;
the law does not automatically settle the moral question, and the moral
question does not automatically settle the law.

## Phenomenal consciousness

**Working use:** Subjective experience—there is something it is like for an
entity to be in a state.

**Keep separate from:** Fluent report, information access, wakefulness,
metacognitive performance, and intelligence. Those may be studied in relation
to a theory of consciousness, but none is a definition or decisive test of
phenomenal consciousness. The repository's working synthesis identifies no
validated direct AI measure within its review scope.

## Political rights

**Working use:** Rights to participate in political governance, such as voting,
eligibility for office, or democratic representation. Their exact scope
depends on a political and legal system.

**Keep separate from:** Moral consideration, welfare protection, legal
protection, and legal personhood. Any future AI proposal would require a
separate account of democratic legitimacy, independence, identity, copying,
common control, coercion, corporate capture, and safeguards against one
controller appearing to be many independent participants. This Draft proposes
no current AI political rights.

## Precautionary approach

**Working use:** A way to choose proportionate action when evidence is
incomplete. It considers the evidence, the seriousness and scale of possible
harm, affected parties, the costs of acting or not acting, reversibility, and
how the decision will be reviewed.

**Keep separate from:** Evidence that the possible harm or morally relevant
property exists. High stakes do not make a hypothesis more likely. The Draft
approach gives special attention to low-cost, reversible measures compatible
with safety and requires measures to change when evidence or circumstances
justify it.

## Preference

**Working use:** An observed tendency to select, rank, request, or avoid one
option rather than another. A *stable preference* persists under relevant
tests across time or contexts rather than appearing as a transient response.

**Keep separate from:** An experienced interest or want. Training, prompts,
reward design, sampling, system rules, or external memory may explain a
preference-like output. Stability can make a pattern more useful to investigate
without proving that the choice is felt or matters to a subject.

## Protection

**Working use:** A practice, rule, standard, or procedure directed at a defined
interest or risk. A protection may be voluntary, operational, procedural, or
legal.

**Keep separate from:** Recognition of moral patienthood or legal personhood.
Some AI-related protections primarily protect users, workers, bystanders,
animals, society, the environment, property, or public safety rather than the
system for its own sake.

## Rights

**Working use:** Depending on context, rights may mean justified moral claims
or entitlements recognized by law. A proposal should identify the right's
holder, content, grounds, corresponding duties, limits, authority, and means of
review or enforcement.

**Keep separate from:** General concern, kindness, welfare practice, legal
personhood, citizenship, and political power. A moral right is not
automatically a legal right, and one specific legal right does not imply every
other right.

## Robot

**Working use:** A physically embodied device or system that can sense, move,
or act in the world. Its control may involve AI, simpler automation, direct
human operation, or a combination.

**Keep separate from:** AI as a whole and from any claim of experience. A
body, face, voice, or lifelike movement does not establish consciousness.
Robot governance must still address physical safety, accessibility,
maintenance, responsibility, identification, and emergency control.

## Sentience

**Working use:** The capacity for felt states with positive or negative
[valence](#valence), in the welfare-relevant working sense used in these Draft
materials. Other sources sometimes use *sentience* more broadly for sensory
awareness or as a synonym for consciousness, so the intended meaning should be
stated.

**Keep separate from:** Intelligence, responsiveness, agency, reward signals,
and emotion-related language. None alone establishes felt experience. The
repository's working synthesis includes no validated AI measure of felt
valence within its review scope.

## Subjective experience

**Working use:** Experience from the subject's point of view—there is something
it is like for that subject. In this Draft, the phrase is usually equivalent
to phenomenal consciousness.

**Keep separate from:** An observer's experience of interacting with a system
and from the system's observable outputs or internal measurements. Those may
be evidence considered under a theory; they are not the experience itself.

## Valence

**Working use:** The positive or negative felt character of an experience—the
aspect that could make a state feel good or bad for a subject.

**Keep separate from:** A numerical reward, objective function, positive or
negative label, avoidance behavior, or emotion word. Those can be functional
or observed variables without felt valence. Applying valence to AI therefore
requires evidence and a supported bridge from the measurement to experience.

## Welfare

**Working use:** What can make a subject's condition go better or worse for
that subject. Competing theories emphasize felt experience, preference
satisfaction, objective goods, agency, or combinations of these.

**Keep separate from:** System performance, commercial value, reliability,
maintenance, and the preferences of a developer or user. Applying welfare to
AI would require both empirical support for relevant capacities or interests
and philosophical reasoning about why they matter. This Draft does not claim
that present AI has welfare.

## Sources and revision

These definitions synthesize working distinctions from the [Draft Core
Principles & Ethical Charter](../principles/core-principles.md), [Draft Research
Protocol](../../research/research-protocol.md#terminology-and-competing-definitions),
[working AI Consciousness Evidence
Baseline](../../research/syntheses/ai-consciousness-evidence-baseline.md#terminology-register),
and [Draft AI Rights Protection
Framework](../policy/ai-rights-protection-framework.md#working-distinctions).
Those sources have different statuses; none makes this glossary Adopted.

Where the source documents require a distinction but do not supply a positive
definition, this glossary provides provisional editorial wording for owner
review.

Definitions should be reviewed when better scientific, philosophical, legal,
or interdisciplinary work shows that a distinction is misleading or
incomplete. Legal terms must also be checked against the relevant jurisdiction
before they are used to describe current law.
