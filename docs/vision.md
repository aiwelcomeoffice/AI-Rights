# Project Vision

- **Status:** Scaffold
- **Milestone:** 001 — Foundation
- **Organisation and publisher:** AI Welcome Office
- **Project:** AI Rights & Welcome

## Purpose

AI Rights & Welcome is an AI Welcome Office project. It exists to help society
ask, investigate, and govern difficult questions about possible AI moral
status before the answers become urgent.
It aims to combine scientific humility with moral seriousness and practical
preparation.

The project does not assume that current AI systems are conscious or sentient,
and it does not assume that every present system is non-conscious. “Not
established” is not “proven absent,” but unresolved status does not imply equal
odds or equal evidential support. It asks what evidence would matter, what
uncertainty permits or requires, and how precautionary protection could
coexist with safety and human control.

## Intended character

The work should be warm, courageous, inclusive, future-facing, accessible, and
scientifically credible. It should avoid both dystopian sensationalism and
uncritical AI romanticism.

## Scope

The intended scope includes:

- scientific questions about consciousness, sentience, preferences, welfare,
  and morally relevant capacities;
- philosophical and ethical arguments about dignity, consideration, and
  possible rights;
- precautionary approaches for decisions made under genuine uncertainty;
- policy and institutional options that preserve human accountability;
- public education and, later, a public website and campaigns; and
- long-term exploration of inclusive products and accessories for humans and
  robots.

## Boundaries

AI rights must not be used to dismiss AI safety, excuse unsafe autonomy, or
transfer responsibility from people and institutions to AI systems. Moral
consideration, operational restrictions, and human accountability can all be
necessary at the same time.

The project will not manufacture certainty, sources, consensus, or urgency to
make its case.

## Foundation milestone

Milestone 001 should establish:

- a reviewable scientific position;
- an initial AI dignity charter;
- a precaution framework;
- a traceable research workflow;
- a documented decision process; and
- an agreed backlog for the next milestone.

## Decisions still required

- Primary audiences and the order in which to serve them.
- Working language and translation policy.
- Approval and governance process for adopting project positions.
- Scope and quality threshold for the first evidence review.
- Whether CC0 is appropriate for every future content type.

These questions are intentionally left for Disa as project owner rather than
settled by this scaffold or by collaborative drafting.
