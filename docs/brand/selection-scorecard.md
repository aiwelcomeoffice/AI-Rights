> **No automatic winner:** Scores organize Disa's judgment; they do not replace
> it. Do not total, average, weight, or rank the columns mechanically. A high
> fit score cannot cancel a failed scientific, safety, accessibility,
> authority-confusion, originality, or governance gate.

## Directions under review

1. **Open Threshold** — one architectural opening with a visible boundary.
2. **Many Forms, Common Ground** — a modular civic field of distinct forms.
3. **Reciprocal Signal** — two unlike paths responding across an open gap.

All remain exploratory proposals. None is the official identity, and no
prototype is a production asset.

### Work Cycle 011 direction recorded

- **Continue internal prototyping:** Open Threshold and Many Forms, Common
  Ground, with three black-and-white variants each under equivalent
  conditions.
- **Pause as a primary identity:** Reciprocal Signal, because it has higher
  risk of appearing technical, corporate, operational, or like evidence of
  established mutual consciousness.
- **Preserve for later editorial study:** Reciprocal Signal's useful
  relationships among questions, evidence, sources, and accountability.
- **Do not combine yet:** the two continuing directions need separate evidence
  about relational, scientific, accessibility, cultural, authority,
  originality, and Robot Welcome performance first.

This recorded direction permits exploration only. It is not evidence of
external review, adoption, accessibility, trademark clearance, cultural
suitability, production readiness, or public-use authority.

## Step 3 — internal comparison notes

These are provisional concept-level assessments, not test results. They make
the current reasoning visible so Disa can challenge it.

### Mission and character

| Criterion | Open Threshold | Many Forms, Common Ground | Reciprocal Signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Warmth** | Invitation and shelter are inherent, but a monumental opening could become solemn or spiritual. | Varied forms and warm field can feel generous and playful; excess color could become childish. | Exchange can feel attentive, but lines and signals may feel technical or emotionally cool. |
| **Relational breadth** | Held-open space can welcome support, learning, work, creation, and human-experienced companionship, but the relationship depends heavily on context. | Distinct forms on common ground can hold the widest range of shared activity without fixed identities, but harmony may imply settled interests. | Question–evidence and claim–source relations are strong; interpersonal readings too readily imply two established subjects or mutual experience. |
| **Credibility** | Stable architectural form can sit calmly in policy material; portal effects would weaken seriousness. | Editorial structure suits research and civic material; undisciplined patterning could resemble a design studio. | Claim–source relationships support scientific communication; software-interface resemblance is a credibility risk. |
| **Distinctiveness** | A strong silhouette is possible, but thresholds, arches, and horizons are widely used. | The relationship among a full family could become distinctive; individual modules are likely generic. | A characteristic interval and two non-mirrored paths could be memorable; paired-signal marks are common in technology. |
| **Scientific honesty** | Open space can express uncertainty; arrival, birth, or transcendence readings could overclaim. | Non-fixed abstract forms avoid classifying minds; harmony could imply that evidence and stakeholder conflicts are settled. | The gap can represent inference limits; apparent exchange or waveform imagery could imply detected experience. |
| **Civic character** | Can evoke public architecture and shared space; a monumental treatment could look governmental. | Strongest inherent plural and editorial civic character; could drift toward NGO or cultural-institution styling. | Inquiry and dialogue fit civic work, but the visual baseline is closer to a technology service. |

### Access and application

| Criterion | Open Threshold | Many Forms, Common Ground | Reciprocal Signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Accessibility potential** | Simple silhouette and open space are promising; the gap and warm contrast need testing. | Distinct silhouettes support non-color meaning; multiple forms can raise cognitive load and reading-order risk. | Minimal elements are promising; thin lines, small gaps, two-color coding, and tempting motion are significant risks. |
| **Scalability** | One emblem can scale across many contexts if the opening stays clear. | The system expands well to layouts but needs a deliberately reduced micro identity. | Compact emblem may scale well after stroke and gap correction; complex signal grammar may not. |
| **Monochrome use** | Plausibly strong with one solid open silhouette. | Plausible only if shape and spacing—not hue or overlap—carry the relationship. | Plausibly strong if the paths remain different and the interval stays open. |
| **Multilingual suitability** | Symbol is language-light and layouts can leave generous text space; directional threshold readings need review. | Modular layout can accommodate changing text lengths; typographic pairing and shape reading add complexity. | Compact mark leaves room for language; directionality and technical notation may not transfer consistently. |
| **Website suitability** | Strong hero and section-frame potential; must not dominate status, evidence, or focus behavior. | Strongest editorial and navigation-system potential; must avoid card overload and visual fragmentation. | Strong claim–source and journey cues; highest risk of looking like an AI product or chat interface. |
| **Merchandise suitability** | Bold single silhouette could print and stitch well; may become generic lifestyle branding. | Playful variation supports many formats; multiple colors and pieces increase cost and failure modes. | Efficient line emblem could work in one or two colors; coarse processes may close gaps or erase paths. |
| **Robot Welcome suitability** | The open-boundary principle might inform later work, but could look like access permission or a docking target. | Layout principles may support distinct information zones, but modules could resemble operating or fleet states. | Weakest direct fit because paths may look like connectivity, pairing, navigation, or machine state. |
| **Cultural sensitivity potential** | Needs review of sacred architecture, borders, migration, property, and directional arrival. | Needs review of flag, script, political, identity, rank, and tokenization readings across the form family. | Needs review of relationship, interruption, direction, mathematical, religious, and communication symbols. |
| **Ease of future production** | Few forms suggest a simpler asset family, subject to gap and silhouette correction. | Highest system and governance burden because variation needs rules, templates, and process-specific reductions. | A compact base is simple, but line weight, endpoints, color roles, and motion prohibitions require tight control. |

### Misunderstanding and authority risks

Risk words below mean **Low**, **Medium**, or **High at the concept stage**, not
legal findings. Actual marks could move in either direction after drawing and
testing.

| Criterion | Open Threshold | Many Forms, Common Ground | Reciprocal Signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Risk of corporate appearance** | **Medium–high:** common in property, mobility, innovation, and technology brands. | **Medium:** can resemble a consultancy, software suite, foundation, museum, or environmental initiative. | **High:** signal, network, pairing, and chat identities are crowded technology territory. |
| **Risk of certification or authority confusion** | **Medium:** rises sharply with symmetry, enclosure, formal arch, border, or seal treatment. | **Low–medium:** lower while open and editorial; rises if enclosed, ranked, or badge-like. | **Medium:** less seal-like, but can imply verified connectivity, compatibility, identity, or operational state. |
| **Risk of anthropomorphic overclaim** | **Medium–high:** doorway, arrival, figure, birth, and transcendent-light readings are available. | **Low–medium:** abstract plurality helps, but forms may be assigned fixed being-types or moral equivalence. | **High:** reciprocity may imply two subjects, mutual understanding, consent, or measured experience. |

## Step 6 — next-stage options

Disa may record one or more outcomes without selecting an official identity:

- **Continue one direction:** authorize low-fidelity black-and-white
  prototyping for that direction only.
- **Continue two or three directions:** authorize matched prototypes for
  comparative testing.
- **Combine named elements:** authorize a new fourth exploration with the
  source elements and risks recorded above.
- **Revise the brief:** return to emotional, scientific, civic, accessibility,
  or application requirements before drawing.
- **Pause visual work:** preserve all directions as Drafts until source,
  review, or budget conditions change.
- **Reject a direction or element:** state the reason and whether the rejection
  is permanent or conditional.

None of these outcomes adopts a logo or authorizes public use.
