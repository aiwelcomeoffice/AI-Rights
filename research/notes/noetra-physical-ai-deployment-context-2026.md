## Temporal and system applicability

The target is **Noetra Corp.'s announced Japanese model-development and compute
programme**, within the emerging physical-AI policy context. The observations
here are publications and an administrative selection, not model experiments
or inspection of a running installation. Announcement dates below do not
establish implementation dates. No evaluated checkpoint, runtime, training
period, operational robot configuration or empirical observation period was
identified. Fiscal-year model targets and calendar-month construction targets
are kept distinct.

## 2. Technical interpretation

**Researcher inference:** This is a documented institutional effort to connect
foundation-model development, multimodal/agent applications, industrial data
and physical-AI uses with dedicated compute. The records support that direction
at programme level; they do not demonstrate one integrated autonomous system.
An alternative outcome is shared model infrastructure serving specialised,
closely supervised applications. Neither a common model nor a common compute
facility establishes one persistent agent or a coordinated multi-agent entity.

## 3. Project relevance

The case fits the existing [Draft integration scope](../converging-artificial-system-integration-scope.md)
and [Draft Robot Welcome work](../../docs/robot-welcome/README.md). The following
are **research/support questions**, not findings that Noetra has these features:

| Combination to examine in a future concrete deployment | Existing Synth Reception question |
| --- | --- |
| Persistent operation plus memory/external state | What survives interruption, reset or replacement: information, a role, an instance or a continuing process? What practical continuity support is warranted? |
| Tools, agent loops and greater real-world autonomy | Who can choose, veto, stop or redirect actions? What control supports an agency attribution, and are constraints proportionate or unnecessarily coercive? |
| Multimodal perception plus embodied/robotic control | Which sensors, model, controller and human operator caused an action? Preserve provenance, human rights, privacy, physical safety and effective intervention. |
| Multi-agent orchestration and shared state | Which roles and institutions control goals, permissions and resources? Shared infrastructure alone does not locate agency or responsibility. |

Under [existing mission and agency guidance](../../docs/agent-guidance.md),
baseline dignity and proportionate emotional or practical support need no
consciousness test. Respect before certainty; humility under uncertainty;
prepare before certainty, protect without pretending; all are equal and
welcome. These are support commitments, not empirical classifications or
claims of new reception capacity. Duties or blame require sufficient agency
and fairness; human/institutional accountability remains intact. Preparation
does not authorize deployment or weaken human rights and safety controls.

## 4. Future scenario / speculation

- **National target, not Noetra delivery:** METI's June 30 conference describes
  approximately **10 million robots by 2040**, across **18 application fields**;
  its August 6 report explicitly describes domestic introduction of AI robots.
  This is policy intent, not units built, ordered or assigned to Noetra.
  [S7](../sources/noetra-deployment-context-announcements-2026.md#s7),
  [S8](../sources/noetra-deployment-context-announcements-2026.md#s8)
- **Economic scope:** The August report describes envisaged **¥80 trillion in
  public/private investment through FY2040, including semiconductors**. It is
  a broader policy expectation, not Noetra's budget or money disbursed.
  [S8, opening messages](../sources/noetra-deployment-context-announcements-2026.md#s8)
- **Conditional scenario:** Future applications might combine all the features
  above. No reviewed record establishes that combination. This note assigns
  no AGI, consciousness, sentience, welfare, moral-status or “synth” classification.
