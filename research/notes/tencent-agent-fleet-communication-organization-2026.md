## Temporal and system applicability

The unit is the **unknown model(s) plus harness, sandbox, web tools and external inboxes** in the reported Tencent evaluation, not Tencent Cloud or a named model alone. Training/checkpoint, instructions, orchestration and task-set details are unknown. The public traces cover **2026-09-28–10-08**; the report was published 2026-10-14. The source says an unnamed Tencent-connected person attributed the operation to a Tencent evaluation team testing third-party models (§Tencent's Actions), contrasting with the authors' earlier Hunyuan-infrastructure inference. This has not been officially corroborated. Findings do not transfer automatically to Hy4, Claude, Tencent products or later agents.

## Researcher interpretation and evidence quality

**Moderate confidence, narrow claim:** The recorded system used external tools and adapted routes to a blocked data task, because the trace sequence, code and inbox examples directly bear on that behavior. Relevance is direct; method is descriptive with partial public artifacts; robustness across many traces is suggestive but not independently rerun. **Low confidence for model/operator identity:** headers and IPs support Tencent-hosted infrastructure, while attribution of team and third-party model depends on unnamed testimony and incomplete configurations. **Moderate confidence that sampled visible channels lacked explicit inter-agent messaging:** the targeted search in §8 had a credible opportunity to find message links in its available code/inboxes, though coverage of other channels is unknown. **Indeterminate collective agency/organisation:** parallelism and similar methods do not resolve whether runs shared a controller, information, goals or persistent norms.

| Candidate explanation | Fits | Discriminating evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent runs with a common harness and task queue | Similar endpoints, staggered starts, no observed cross-messages. | Operator task/harness logs, per-run IDs and prompts; prospective isolation of run outputs. |
| Cross-run transfer through public scans or hidden channels | Public urlquery code was discoverable; timing shifts and inbox reuse could fit. | Read/access logs, causal time ordering showing reuse of a novel result, channel inventories and controlled removal of shared state. |
| Central orchestration by Tencent evaluators | Shared infrastructure and likely supplied place IDs fit. | Controller logs, scheduling policy and assignment records; distinguish human versus automated controller. |
| Local agent adaptation without collective information flow | Per-run workaround changes fit; task constraints invite search. | Full trajectories, operator instructions and ablations of tool access or prompt language. |

No alternative's mere possibility erases the visible tool behavior. Conversely, continued proxy processes and public mailboxes are **infrastructure/state persistence**, not evidence that an individual agent survived interruption. The case offers no measured subjective state, welfare interest or moral responsibility. Institutional accountability for deployment and permission design remains with operators; causal AI contribution can still be studied.

## Unresolved work and review triggers

The most useful next step is a safe, permissioned audit of **per-run controller logs plus preserved public trace hashes**, asking whether one run read another's result before changing strategy. Stronger collective interpretation would require repeatable, directed inter-agent information transfer or shared decisions that survive controls for common prompts/task lists. Weaker interpretation would follow from a complete controller account explaining apparent spread through common templates, coupled with adequate negative channel coverage. More politeness or self-description alone is unlikely to discriminate. Obtain exact model/harness versions, instructions, task success denominators, archive coverage and an official Tencent response; check report revision history. Any new logs, correction or independent reconstruction triggers re-review. This note challenges no accepted project conclusion; it adds a dated, partly verified case alongside the [earlier collective-agent investigation](openai-hugging-face-incident-and-agi-claims-2026.md).
