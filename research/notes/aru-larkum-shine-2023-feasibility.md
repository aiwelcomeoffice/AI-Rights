## What the source actually says

### Question or proposition

The authors ask whether contemporary or near-term AI-consciousness claims are
defensible when evaluated against neuroscience (abstract and review body; exact
locators TODO: verify).

### Methods or reasoning

They compare LLM/artificial-network organization with properties of conscious
biological systems, emphasizing embodied and embedded input, thalamocortical
organization, cellular dynamics, and evolutionary/developmental histories.

### Reported findings

The authors conclude that 2023-era systems lack features they regard as
important and that current/near-term consciousness claims are difficult to
defend.

### Negative, null, mixed, or contrary material

The argument identifies disanalogies, not a validated negative measurement. It
does not show that all non-biological systems are incapable of experience or
that every cited biological feature is necessary.

### Authors' conclusions and caveats

The skeptical conclusion is grounded in incomplete neuroscience and a
biologically oriented theory of what features matter.

## Researcher's interpretation

This source has independent value against loose “artificial neural network =
brain” reasoning. It should weigh against strong behavioral/analogy claims but
not be converted into a proof that current or future AI cannot be conscious.

## Criticisms and methodological concerns

- Brain dissimilarity may be irrelevant under multiple realizability.
- The paper's AI snapshot ages rapidly and lacks proprietary runtime access.
- Constitutive features are not cleanly separated from biological correlates.
- No controlled system comparison or validated outcome is available.

## Unanswered questions

- Which biological properties are necessary, sufficient, or contingent?
- Can non-biological systems implement relevant dynamics differently?
- How do later multimodal, recurrent, embodied, and agentic systems compare?

## What would change this interpretation

- **Evidence that would strengthen it:** Causal evidence that proposed
  biological dynamics are necessary across conscious biological cases and
  absent in audited AI.
- **Evidence that would weaken it:** Validated substrate-independent indicators
  or artificial implementations producing distinctive theory predictions.
- **Evidence unlikely to resolve it:** More humanlike dialogue alone.
- **Re-review triggers:** New architectures, direct author reply, replication,
  correction, or stronger substrate-dependence evidence.
