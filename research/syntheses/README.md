# Research Syntheses

Store comparisons across multiple sources and evidence lineages here. Follow
the [Draft research protocol](../research-protocol.md) and copy the
[research synthesis template](_template.md) for each new synthesis.

A synthesis must state its question, scope, definitions, method, included and
excluded evidence, dependencies, evidence-quality profile, confidence,
limitations, and review status. It must include relevant negative, null, and
contradictory findings and preserve material disagreement rather than forcing
consensus. Do not call a document a systematic review or meta-analysis unless
its documented method meets the protocol's requirements.

Syntheses remain working material until reviewed for publication under
[`docs/research/`](../../docs/research/README.md).
