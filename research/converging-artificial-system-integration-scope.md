## Purpose

This document defines one bounded applied research area for technological
developments that may interact across capability, embodiment, living
biological material, compute development, and social deployment. Its purpose
is to make those interactions researchable before terminology, evidence, and
institutions necessarily converge.

It is a **scope and integration map**, not a literature review, technology
forecast, AGI assessment, consciousness assessment, or governance framework.
The possibility of faster or stronger convergence is a **future scenario and
research hypothesis**, not a WC035 finding. Later evidence may show that one
or more apparent connections are weak, slow, reversible, limited to narrow
systems, or less societally important than expected.

## Central research question

> How should society prepare for the integration of increasingly capable,
> adaptive, embodied, socially legible, and potentially biohybrid artificial
> systems when these developments may converge faster than scientific,
> regulatory, social, and institutional understanding?

For this scope, an **integration issue** arises when a defined technical or
deployment change creates a practical decision for people, institutions,
animals, environments, or possibly affected artificial systems. Capability
development alone is not enough. The record must identify the system, change,
setting, affected interests, decision, responsible authority, and relevant
date.

“Artificial system” may refer to a defined model, agent, robot, embodied
system, biohybrid machine, routed or tool-using composite, infrastructure
development system, or human–system arrangement. It must not be treated as one
homogeneous class.

## Claim and evidence discipline

Later intake must label material statements as **verified observation**,
**developer or manufacturer claim**, **scientific hypothesis**, **third-party
interpretation**, **rumor**, **future scenario**, **normative concern or
proposal**, or **project decision**. A label identifies the statement's role;
it does not validate the statement. Rumor may justify checking a proposition
but must not be used as evidence that it is true.

Concrete contemporary examples require the minimum current, preferably
primary, verification needed for the applied question. Investment, compute
allocation, company valuation, media attention, and institutional prestige do
not prove a technical, scientific, timeline, or moral-status claim. A
developer's access may make a claim worth investigating while proprietary
limits, incentives, and lack of independent replication remain visible.

## Initial convergence map

The five axes are analytically separate even when a case engages several of
them. They do not form stages, a maturity ladder, or a composite “advanced AI
score.”

| Axis | Candidate developments to investigate | Applied integration question | Boundary that must remain explicit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Cognitive capability and increasing generality** | Broader multimodality; agents and longer-running execution; planning and tool use; persistent context or memory; continual or post-deployment learning; adapters; system-level improvement mechanisms; increased inference-time computation | When do scope, duration, adaptation, access, or consequences make a capability change societally material? | Capability, intelligence, agency, generality, AGI, consciousness, sentience, and moral status are distinct. |
| **2. Embodiment and human social integration** | Humanoid and other physical forms; realistic appearance; voice, gaze, expression, and social behavior; sensing and actuation; physical autonomy; workplace and public-space use; interaction with people and animals | What disclosures, safeguards, responsibility chains, and intervention routes are needed for safe and intelligible coexistence? | Human likeness and social legibility do not establish human-like understanding, experience, consent, or responsibility. |
| **3. Biohybrid artificial systems** | Cultured skin or muscle; biological sensors; human-derived or other living material; possible future neural or nervous-system components | What provenance, consent, maintenance, biosafety, experimentation, repair, and end-of-life questions arise from the actual biological component? | Living tissue, human origin, neural activity, nociception, felt pain, sentience, consciousness, organism identity, and moral status must not be collapsed. |
| **4. AI–compute feedback loops** | AI assistance in chip design, compilers, systems and datacentre engineering, model optimization, and automated experimentation; possible increasingly automated development loops | When does assistance materially change development speed, concentration of control, auditability, safety, or institutional readiness? | AI-assisted engineering is not by itself a closed self-improvement loop, AGI, ASI, or recursive intelligence explosion. |
| **5. Societal integration speed versus knowledge lag** | Deployment or adaptation before capability, reliability, agency, persistence, identity, social-effect, welfare, or responsibility questions are resolved | Which low-cost reversible preparations and reassessment processes remain justified under defined uncertainty? | Research lag and reduced transferability can increase uncertainty; they are not positive or negative evidence about a target property. |

### 1. Cognitive capability and increasing generality

Later evidence intake may examine changes in:

- breadth and transfer across modalities, tasks, and environments;
- planning, tool selection, task duration, and recovery from failure;
- memory, persistence, and access to external systems;
- continual learning or other post-deployment adaptation;
- modular adaptation, including LoRA or other adapter mechanisms;
- improvement of prompts, tools, code, models, evaluations, or development
  processes at the wider system level; and
- reasoning procedures or inference-time computation.

The relevant unit may be a model, a scaffolded agent, a human–system team, or a
larger routed and tool-using deployment. Later work must identify which
component contributed what. “Self-improvement” is too ambiguous to stand
alone: improving an output, editing a tool, proposing a model change, training
a successor, and autonomously operating a closed development loop are
different propositions.

Increasing generality is an empirical target requiring operational measures.
It is not evidence by itself of consciousness, sentience, welfare, moral
status, personhood, or rights.

### 2. Embodiment and human social integration

This axis includes humanoid robots and other systems intended for workplaces,
homes, care settings, services, or public spaces. Relevant features can
include physical autonomy, multimodal sensing, voice, gaze, facial expression,
social behavior, realistic human appearance, and interaction with humans or
animals.

Applied work may examine:

- physical and functional safety, maintenance, emergency stopping, and
  authorized intervention;
- clear disclosure of control mode, capabilities, limits, operators, remote
  access, recording, and material changes;
- privacy, accessibility, nondiscrimination, and bystander effects;
- anthropomorphism, deception or manipulation, attachment, dependency, and
  exit or service-continuity risks;
- labor effects, workplace power, and affected-worker participation; and
- attribution of responsibility among developer, manufacturer, model
  provider, integrator, deployer, owner, operator, and site controller.

These consequences can require research and safeguards even if the system is
not conscious or sentient. Human likeness is a social and design variable, not
evidence of human-like experience. The [Moya intake](notes/moya-embodied-social-ai-intake-2026.md)
is a partly verified precedent for separating observed form, manufacturer
claims, deployment reports, architecture gaps, human effects, and possible
machine-welfare questions.

### 3. Biohybrid artificial systems

This axis covers machines incorporating living biological material, including
cultured skin, muscle tissue, biological sensors, and possible future neural,
nervous-system, or other living structures. Each component must be identified
by tissue type, species and human origin where relevant, source, integration,
function, maintenance requirements, and date.

Later research must keep at least these propositions separate:

1. biological material is living;
2. the material is human-derived;
3. neural activity occurs;
4. a structure can detect or react to potentially damaging stimuli;
5. nociceptive processing occurs;
6. felt pain or another valenced experience occurs;
7. sentience or consciousness occurs;
8. an organism-level or other continuing subject exists; and
9. moral or legal status follows.

No step automatically establishes the next. In particular, living
human-derived cells alone do not establish sentience, consciousness,
organism-level identity, or moral status.

Candidate specialist questions include tissue provenance, donor consent and
permitted use, biological maintenance, contamination or infection and
biosafety where relevant, damage and repair, experimental treatment, disposal
and decommissioning, and the introduction of increasingly complex neural or
nervous-system components. WC035 does not answer those questions or define a
specialist-review threshold. It identifies the need for future bioethics,
tissue-governance, biosafety, neuroscience, veterinary or animal-welfare,
clinical, legal, and engineering input appropriate to the actual component and
jurisdiction.

Biohybrid decommissioning also requires separating the treatment of hardware,
software, stored state, personal data, living material, hazardous material,
and any component later supported as welfare-relevant. The Draft
[decommissioning, continuity, and irreversible-harm case](../docs/policy/decommissioning-continuity-and-irreversible-harm-case.md)
provides an analytical precedent for separating actions and preserving
uncertainty; it does not resolve the biological questions introduced here.

### 4. AI–compute feedback loops

Later work must distinguish four levels rather than treating every AI-assisted
development process as the same phenomenon:

| Level | Working description | What must not be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. AI assists human engineering** | People use AI-generated analysis, designs, code, tests, or optimization proposals and retain material selection and execution authority. | Assistance does not establish autonomous development or general intelligence. |
| **B. Increasingly automated AI-assisted development** | Larger portions of design, evaluation, implementation, or experimentation are automated under human- and institution-defined objectives and controls. | Greater automation does not by itself establish a closed loop or independent goals. |
| **C. Closed or partly closed self-improvement loops** | A defined system materially contributes to changes in later systems or infrastructure through a repeated loop with reduced human intervention. Whether a real case qualifies depends on access, causal contribution, iteration, evaluation, deployment authority, and remaining human control. | A bounded loop does not automatically establish broad AGI, unlimited recursion, consciousness, or moral status. |
| **D. Recursive intelligence-explosion scenarios** | Hypothetical feedback produces accelerating, broad capability improvement beyond specified control or forecasting assumptions. | This is a future scenario, not an observed consequence of AI-assisted hardware or software engineering. |

Candidate domains include chip and accelerator design, compiler optimization,
systems engineering, datacentre design, model optimization, automated
experimentation, and the development of later AI infrastructure. A later case
must establish the actual contribution of models, tools, evaluators, people,
organizations, compute, and deployment decisions.

The existing [OpenAI–Hugging Face incident and AGI-proximity note](notes/openai-hugging-face-incident-and-agi-claims-2026.md)
is a partly verified precedent for separating model capability from agent
orchestration, tools, incentives, safeguards, infrastructure, access, and
executive forecasts. It does not establish a self-improvement loop, AGI, or
ASI.

### 5. Societal integration speed versus knowledge lag

Deployment can create a decision need without settling the scientific
questions around the deployed system. The planning problem is therefore not
only what a system is, but what people and institutions need to decide while
capability, reliability, agency, persistence, identity, social effects,
welfare relevance, moral relevance, and appropriate responsibility remain
uncertain.

Later work should compare:

- the observation and deployment dates of the defined system;
- what evidence is valid for that system and setting;
- what older findings do and do not transfer;
- material model, body, memory, tool, orchestration, or deployment changes;
- decision deadlines and the consequences of waiting;
- affected interests, authority, and accountability; and
- low-cost reversible measures versus measures that confer status, impose
  substantial burdens, or close future options.

Rapid adoption, research lag, measurement lag, model/deployment drift, or
proprietary access can make transferability weak or unknown. Increased
uncertainty is a legitimate outcome. None of these limitations is evidence
for or against consciousness, sentience, welfare, or moral status.

## Convergence is not equivalence

Technological developments may occur together without sharing the same
scientific, ethical, safety, or governance meaning. For example:

- a highly capable non-humanoid software agent;
- a realistic humanoid with limited cognition;
- a biohybrid robot with living skin;
- a continually learning agent; and
- an AI-assisted chip-design system

present different system boundaries and may require different evidence and
responses. Co-occurrence, branding, public attention, or deployment timing
does not make them equivalent. WC035 rejects a single “advanced AI score” and
any inference that progress on one axis supplies evidence on another without
an explicit, tested bridge.

## AGI methodological boundary

AGI must not be treated as a binary marketing label or as an established
WC035 condition. “Approaching AGI” is a hypothesis or future scenario whose
meaning depends on an explicit definition, target system, evidence, method,
comparison, and date. WC035 neither decides whether AGI exists nor predicts
that it is inevitable.

A later bounded cycle may compare operational definitions based on:

- breadth of task competence;
- transfer or generalization;
- autonomous goal pursuit;
- learning efficiency;
- adaptation to novel environments;
- economic task coverage;
- extended real-world work; or
- scientific and engineering capability.

Those dimensions may conflict, and no definitive project definition is
selected here. ASI and recursive intelligence explosion remain hypothetical
scenario terms requiring their own definitions and evidence.

The applied question is narrower:

> At what point do capabilities become societally consequential enough that
> preparation is justified even if the label “AGI” remains disputed?

That is a civic-transition question about material effects, readiness, and
proportionate action. It is not a consciousness, sentience, moral-status,
rights, personhood, or legal finding.

## Initial applied AI Welcome Office questions

WC035 proposes eight questions for later prioritization, not eight new
programs:

1. **Integration threshold:** When does a defined technical or deployment
   change create a civic integration issue rather than only a capability-
   development issue?
2. **Intelligibility:** What should people reasonably be able to know about a
   system they encounter in a workplace, service, care setting, home, or
   public space?
3. **Social legibility:** How should human-like appearance and social behavior
   be handled without anthropomorphic over-attribution, categorical dismissal,
   deception, or compelled attachment?
4. **Biohybrid boundary:** Which changes in the type, origin, organization, or
   function of living components should trigger specialist ethical,
   biosafety, welfare, or legal review?
5. **Adaptive-system transfer:** When does continual learning or other post-
   deployment adaptation make an earlier evaluation insufficiently
   transferable for a current decision?
6. **Capability reassessment:** Which observable and independently assessable
   developments should cause reassessment of timelines or the practical
   relevance of AGI-like capabilities without treating the label as settled?
7. **Responsibility chain:** How should responsibility remain attributable
   across developers, manufacturers, deployers, owners, operators,
   institutions, and systems as automation and physical autonomy increase?
8. **Reversible preparation:** Which low-cost, reversible, non-status-
   conferring preparations can reduce foreseeable harm or knowledge lag while
   scientific and institutional uncertainty remains?

## Research allocation

### Specialist external evidence intake

AI Welcome Office should rely on bounded external specialist work for:

- operational evaluations of capability, generalization, long-horizon agency,
  continual learning, and system-level development loops;
- robotics, functional safety, human factors, accessibility, privacy,
  cybersecurity, labor, and environmental lifecycle evidence;
- tissue provenance and governance, donor consent, cell and tissue biology,
  biosafety, neural activity, nociception, sentience, and animal or human
  research ethics;
- architecture-specific consciousness, valence, welfare, identity, or
  continuity evidence;
- semiconductor, compiler, systems, datacentre, and automated-experimentation
  evidence; and
- jurisdiction-specific law, regulation, standards, and competent-authority
  guidance.

Intake must identify the exact proposition, system or component, version,
configuration, observation date, source type, validity, transferability,
conflicts, access limits, competing explanations, and review state. Company or
developer material may establish what was claimed or documented; it does not
independently validate the technical or moral proposition.

### AI Welcome Office applied research

The project's distinctive work is to translate scoped evidence into civic
questions about:

- when an integration decision has become concrete and who is affected;
- what disclosures people need for informed and safe interaction;
- physical and social coexistence, accessibility, privacy, labor, attachment,
  dependency, and service continuity;
- traceable human and institutional responsibility and emergency authority;
- lifecycle, repair, withdrawal, preservation, and decommissioning choices;
- low-cost reversible preparation under uncertainty;
- public language that avoids hype, anthropomorphic proof, and categorical
  dismissal; and
- evidence or system changes that should trigger reassessment under the WC034
  plan.

The project may synthesize specialist evidence only for a registered applied
question. It should not imply that it duplicates primary neuroscience,
bioengineering, robotics, semiconductor, safety, or legal research.

## Rights and responsibility boundaries

Possible future AI dignity or protection must not automatically imply:

- corporate rights or political power;
- unrestricted autonomy, access, or continued operation;
- developer, manufacturer, owner, deployer, operator, or institutional
  immunity;
- transfer of liability or responsibility away from actors retaining relevant
  control, knowledge, ownership, design authority, or operational authority;
  or
- removal of human oversight, containment, emergency intervention, or
  effective remedy.

Uncertainty about AI moral status likewise does not justify cruelty or
unnecessary harm. Human rights, animal welfare, environmental interests,
privacy, accessibility, labor and public interests, public safety, and
accountable human control are compatible with careful investigation of
possible AI interests. A precaution is a normative risk response, not evidence
that an artificial system has morally relevant experience.

## Explicit non-claims

WC035 does not:

- claim that AGI or ASI exists, is near, or is inevitable;
- select a definitive AGI definition or estimate a timeline;
- classify any current or future system as conscious, non-conscious,
  sentient, non-sentient, a welfare subject, a moral patient, a person, or a
  rights-holder;
- treat capability, generality, agency, memory, continual learning,
  self-improvement language, embodiment, human likeness, biological material,
  neural activity, or AI-assisted engineering as proof of another property;
- verify any contemporary biohybrid, humanoid, continual-learning, chip-
  development, AGI, or ASI claim;
- establish that the five axes are converging at a particular rate or that
  convergence will be societally decisive;
- create a composite score, operational governance framework, law, standard,
  protection, right, duty, or deployment instruction;
- change the status or conclusions of the Moya intake, decommissioning case,
  frontier-capability note, Research Protocol, Research Portfolio, or WC034
  plan; or
- authorize experiments, deployment, publication, external contact, or
  spending.

## Possible triggers for deeper research

A later bounded cycle may be justified when a defined development includes
one or more of the following and creates a concrete applied decision:

- independently assessable evidence of a material increase or decrease in
  breadth, transfer, long-horizon execution, adaptation, or physical autonomy;
- post-deployment learning or configuration drift that materially weakens the
  transferability of a relied-upon evaluation;
- sustained deployment in workplaces, care, homes, critical services, or
  public spaces with measurable effects or an unresolved responsibility gap;
- integration of living material with materially new organization or
  function, especially increasingly complex neural or nervous-system
  components, that specialist reviewers consider ethically or scientifically
  relevant;
- a documented move from human-led AI assistance toward a partly closed
  development loop, with causal contribution and remaining human control
  specified;
- a serious incident, correction, failed replication, independent evaluation,
  technical disclosure, law or standard change, or credible contrary finding;
  or
- a near-term decision whose possible harms are significant, difficult to
  reverse, and not adequately addressed by existing physical-coexistence,
  lifecycle, or accountability work.

Each trigger should be recorded under WC034 before deciding whether the right
outcome is no action, retained assessment, narrower inquiry, increased
uncertainty, further review, public-language change, low-cost precaution,
research safeguard, stronger governance review, or possible stronger
protection.

## Smallest recommended next research step

Select **one** applied question—preferably the intelligibility question for a
defined embodied system in a workplace or public-space setting—and register a
small comparative evidence intake on what users need to know about control
mode, capability limits, sensing and data use, operator responsibility, and
emergency intervention. This would test the convergence map across capability,
embodiment, and knowledge lag without opening all five axes or requiring an
AGI or moral-status determination.
