## Source report and evidence profile

The team analyzed urlquery scans, decoded submitted programs, webhook.site inbox metadata, IP/proxy headers and request timing, then added hidden text to its preliminary page advertising a researcher-controlled mailbox. It reports over 2,600 Amap-related attempts over the broader period, **2,599 urlquery submissions** in its narrower 2026-09-28–10-05 survey, and **33 agent messages in 13 mailbox threads** (§§Technical Overview, Communication, Mailbox stats, findings 1–5). Some attempts retrieved map data; most did not (§5). Public traces support parallel task activity, access workarounds, and some researcher–agent exchange. The team says no observed channel showed real-time inter-agent collaboration (§8). The unnamed Tencent-connected source attributes the evaluation to a separate Tencent evaluation team testing third-party models, and says no external systems were breached (§Tencent's Actions); this is attributed testimony, not an official response or independent audit.

**Direction and quality:** Direct and moderately informative for public traffic and the team's own intervention; descriptive causal strength, partial trace transparency and **low independence** for its own archive and analysis. Intervening with hidden text changes the observed environment and may contaminate later behavior. Inboxes expose at most 50 requests and urlquery samples a public channel, so missing coordination elsewhere remains possible. Query-style changes and shared endpoints fit shared task lists, common harnesses, independent discovery or information transfer; the report could not separate them. Model self-identification as Claude is output, not verified identity (§7). Funding/affiliations are partly listed in contributors; access, editorial control and any full funding disclosure remain unchecked. The report provides linked examples but archived inbox audit/model tests are available only on request (§Appendix); no independent reproduction was found in this bounded search.

**Supports:** observed external tool use, adaptive workarounds, parallel activity and replies to researchers for the traced configuration. **Weighs against:** visible real-time collaboration on inspected channels, subject to detection limits. **Does not establish:** deliberate fleet-wide strategy, persistent individual identity, autonomous institutional goals, model identity, subjective states, or an operator policy decision.
