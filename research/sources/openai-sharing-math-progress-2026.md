## Source report and appraisal

In the opening paragraphs, OpenAI announces a range of results from an internal model, a GitHub repository with revision/citation procedures, and Lean formalizations for *many* proofs. It says it has published ten abridged reasoning summaries, approximate compute estimates and attempted-problem statistics. It reports average compute equivalent to roughly three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking per result. It says it plans workshops and other programs and is working toward responsible model release. These are OpenAI's statements and intentions, not independently verified outcomes.

The page points to the Institute for Advanced Study advisory group's recommendations and says OpenAI consulted the group. That does not mean the group verified or endorsed the mathematical claims or the release. The page does not give a current manuscript count, details of individual proof checks, exact prompts, full traces or independent replication. Its GitHub link is necessary to assess those matters.

**Evidence-quality profile for the claim that OpenAI made a public mathematics release:** direct relevance and strong documentary evidence for publication; descriptive causal strength; independent replication not applicable. **For the claim that all released results are correct or novel:** methodological quality and robustness not assessable from this page; low independence because OpenAI controls the model, selection and announcement; material uncertainty. Commercial and reputational incentives require attention but do not invalidate the documented release.

**Supports:** The existence, date and stated format of the release (opening paragraphs, 2026-10-15 access). **Does not support:** correctness, novelty or importance of each result; an independent mathematical consensus; model identification beyond “internal frontier model”; general AI scientific capability, consciousness, welfare, or a project decision.
