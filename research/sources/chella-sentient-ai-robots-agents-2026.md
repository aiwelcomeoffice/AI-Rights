## Source claim and method

Sections 3 and 6 separate evidence about a robot from human attribution and argue that embodiment creates testable sensorimotor questions while increasing projection risk. Sections 5, 7, and 8 propose multi-theory, domain-specific indicators and causal interventions. Section 9.2 proposes, without performing, matched physical/simulated/disembodied care-robot comparisons, reversible sensorimotor and memory perturbations, valence-like functional tests, and separate user-attribution measures. Section 10 proposes staged safeguards. The author explicitly says that causal evidence for a functional indicator does not establish phenomenal consciousness (§8.7).

The paper reports **no robot sample, experiment, outcome, null result, or replication** for its care-robot protocol. Its evidence levels are proposed conventions; the note does not adopt them as validated thresholds. A general performance loss after a lesion would not identify the mechanism claimed; §9.2 asks for selective deficits against matched controls.

## Claim-specific appraisal

**Claim assessed:** A testable, robot-specific welfare research design can be formulated. **Direction:** Supports a design proposal, not welfare status. Relevance is direct; method is conceptual and detailed but unvalidated; replication is not applicable; causal strength is a proposal only; discriminating value is prospective; robustness is untested; uncertainty is material. Alternative explanations named include scripting, reward optimization, language training, external wrappers, and human projection. Academic affiliation and disclosures are visible; independent assessment of the author's proposal is not performed here. Earlier indicator and welfare papers are cited, so this is not an independent empirical confirmation of them.

**Supports:** description of a possible controlled research design (§§6–10). **Challenges:** an inference from appearance or self-report alone (§§3–4). **Does not support:** any claim that a named humanoid is sentient, has welfare, or has passed the proposed tests. **TODO: verify:** independent expert appraisal of construct validity, safe experimental design, correction status, and whether a completed application exists.
