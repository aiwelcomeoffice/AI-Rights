## Source report and evidence profile

In “Staring into the black hole,” Clark estimated around **60%** probability of automated AI R&D by end-2028, defined there as a frontier model autonomously training a successor, and **30%** for 2027. He identified research creativity as a reason not to make 2027 the modal year at that time. Earlier sections assembled coding, agent time-horizon, AI R&D engineering, automated alignment research and mathematics examples; these are a narrative mosaic with disparate evaluation setups, not a single experiment. The October statement changes his modal year but does not publish replacement numerical probabilities. This source verifies the historical comparison only; it does not validate individual benchmark claims, a complete autonomy loop or forecast calibration.
