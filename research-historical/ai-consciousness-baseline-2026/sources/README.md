# Historical Source Records — AI Consciousness Baseline 2026

These 21 partly verified working source records form the dated source registry
for the [historical AI Consciousness Evidence Baseline](../ai-consciousness-evidence-baseline.md):
20 core records and one low-weight supplementary valence map. They retain
their own statuses, protocol lineage, review limitations, and source-specific
validity boundaries. Inclusion is not endorsement.

For empirical AI material, evidence about a studied system, observed
deployment, or concrete system event whose relevant target predates 2026 is
historical for current-system applicability. Later publication or record-
update dates do not make that target contemporary. Conceptual, philosophical,
and human-neuroscience material retains the relevance supported by its own
methods and claims.
